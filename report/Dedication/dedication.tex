\begin{center}
\setcode{utf8}

\begin{arabtext}
الحمد لله الذي بنعمته تتم الصالحات
\end{arabtext}
\setcode{arabtex}

\medskip
\textit{All praise be to God, by whose grace all good things are accomplished.}
\end{center}

\vspace{1cm}
\begin{flushright}
\itshape

To my loving parents, my twin pillars and my role models, for their
everlasting love and unwavering sacrifices, who gave everything to help me
become the best version of myself. Thank you for everything you have given
me and for always being there. None of this would have been possible
without your wonderful support and endless sacrifice.

\medskip
To my brother and sisters, for their endless encouragement and their trust
in me.

\medskip
To all my friends, for having sparked my journey from the very
beginning, and for the constant encouragement that made all of this
possible.

\medskip
To the INSAT community, which shaped parts of who I am and gave me
countless unforgettable memories in the last five years.

\medskip
To the JCI~INSAT family: thanks to you, I discovered new potentials in
myself and developed values, experiences and friendships that shaped my professional
and personal identity. You helped bring out the best in me, and I will
always be grateful.

\bigskip
\textbf{Thank you for everything.}

\end{flushright}
