This is one of the most important and yet most neglected parts of the report.
What you should \underline{not} do here is talk about how rewarding the
internship was, how it taught you to integrate into a team, what the working
world is like, and so on.\\
Frankly, nobody cares -- at least not in this section. That kind of material
belongs in the Acknowledgements or in the Dedication.\\
A conclusion is simple: first, it summarizes what you described in the
report -- your contribution, the tools you used, your methodology. You can
also mention the difficulties you encountered. Second, it lays out
perspectives: features that could be added to your application, directions
for future improvement.
